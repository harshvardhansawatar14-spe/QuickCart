
QUICKCART POWER BI DATA MODEL
=============================

DIMENSIONS
----------

dim_customers
    CustomerID -> fact_orders.CustomerID

dim_products
    ProductID -> fact_order_items.ProductID

dim_date
    Date -> fact_orders.OrderDate

dim_location
    City / State -> fact_orders.City / State


FACT TABLES
-----------

fact_orders
fact_order_items
fact_payments
fact_returns
fact_deliveries
fact_inventory
